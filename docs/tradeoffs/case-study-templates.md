To make your "Master Capability Statement" even more persuasive, you need to show, not just tell. Since you are solving a technical "bottleneck" problem, your case studies should follow a **"Friction-to-Flow"** narrative.

Here are three templates tailored to the specific types of "performance failures" you’ve identified.

---

### Template 1: The "Thermal Throttling" Recovery

**Focus:** Proving you can unlock the compute power the customer already paid for.

* **The Problem:** A high-end [Robot Type] was experiencing 70% performance drops within 15 minutes of deployment due to thermal saturation of the [Chipset Name].
* **The Branes Intervention:** We deployed our Agentic system to map the thermal-to-compute overhead. We identified that the autonomous application was mismanaging [Specific Resource, e.g., GPU cycles], leading to wasted energy.
* **The Result:** By optimizing the "Body-to-Brain" interface, we eliminated throttling, allowing the system to maintain **100% of its design-point performance** indefinitely.
* **The Value:** 3x increase in real-time data processing without hardware changes.

---

### Template 2: The "Persistence" Extension

**Focus:** Increasing mission distance and battery life through efficiency.

* **The Problem:** An autonomous [Drone/UGV] was failing to meet its 4-hour mission requirement, consistently returning to base at the 2.5-hour mark due to inefficient energy draws.
* **The Branes Intervention:** Our system identified "Energy Leaks" in the path-planning and sensor-fusion algorithms that were over-taxing the hardware.
* **The Result:** We re-engineered the functional requirements for energy efficiency, extending the mission time by **45%**.
* **The Value:** The client achieved "Full Mission Success" for the first time, reducing the fleet size needed to cover the same territory.

---

### Template 3: The "Edge Capture" Optimization

**Focus:** Performance engineering for high-information environments.

* **The Problem:** A robotic inspection system was failing to capture high-resolution imagery because the compute system couldn't handle the AI inference load and the data write-speed simultaneously.
* **The Branes Intervention:** Using the Branes.ai Embodied AI Agent, we balanced the operational load between the CPU and the AI accelerator.
* **The Result:** Information capture density increased by **4x**, with zero system crashes reported in the field.
* **The Value:** High-fidelity "Actionable Intelligence" delivered at a fraction of the previous energy cost.

---

### How to use these in your Podcast/Meetings:

When the host asks for an example, don’t name names (unless you have permission). Instead, say:

> *"We recently looked at a system where the customer was only getting 10% of their promised compute power. Here is how we stepped in..."*

**Pro-Tip:** Keep a "Library of Failure" folder. Every time you see a new way a robot fails to meet its design point, jot it down. These become the "Aha!" moments for your future marketing.
